Architecture & Multimodal Fusion Stream

Mô hình dựa trên cơ sở là openai/clip-vit-base-patch32. Kiến trúc được tùy biến dưới dạng Late Fusion Network đa phương thức, sử dụng cơ chế xử lý đồng thời (parallel streams) cho tín hiệu Hình ảnh và Văn bản (Cốt truyện). Các đặc trưng Vector sau khi thu được (512-dim) sẽ được chuẩn hóa cấp độ L2 (L2 Normalization) trước khi hợp nhất xuyên không gian bằng phép nối (Concatenate) thành vector 1024-dim. Thông qua Classifier Head đa tầng cùng hàm thất thoát BCEWithLogitsLoss(pos_weight), mạng tự động trích xuất Tương tác Xuyên chiều (Cross-Modal Interactivity) giải quyết triệt để sự cố sụp đổ trọng F1.

LoRA Integration (Low-Rank Adaptation)

Technical Approach: LoRA surgically alters the forward pass of massive Linear layers (such as the q_proj and v_proj matrices in Self-Attention blocks) by freezing the dense pre-trained weights W0 and introducing low-rank rank-decomposition trainable matrices B and A. The updated forward pass computes h = W_0(x) + (α/r)*B(A(x)). This bypasses computing enormous gradients and isolates knowledge extraction to minimal adapters.

Simplified Intuition: Imagine learning a new painting style. Instead of scraping and repainting an entire canvas (full fine-tuning), LoRA acts like placing a thin, semi-transparent colored film over the masterpiece. You only physically paint on the colored film. This requires vastly less paint (parameters) but perfectly changes the visual tone to accomplish the new task, leaving the original masterpiece entirely intact!

# CLIP LoRA Configuration & Custom Fusion Classifier
lora_config = LoraConfig(
    r=8,
    lora_alpha=16,
    target_modules=["q_proj", "k_proj", "v_proj", "out_proj", "fc1", "fc2", "qkv", "proj"],
    lora_dropout=0.05,
    bias="none",
    task_type=TaskType.FEATURE_EXTRACTION,
    modules_to_save=["classifier_head"]
)

# Classifier Head Projection
concat_features = torch.cat((image_features, text_features), dim=1) # 512 + 512 = 1024

Interpretability (Attention Rollout)

Decoding Attention Rollout Mechanisms

What it is: A visual mapping tool that tracks exactly where the Deep Learning model is "looking" when it makes its final prediction. Instead of treating the network like a black box, it forces the AI to highlight its focal points.

How it works (Technically): Unlike CNN-based Grad-CAM which relies on gradient backward passes, Attention Rollout iteratively traverses the attention matrices across multiple Transformer blocks. It aggregates the raw self-attention heads, accounts for the residual identity connections I to track preserved spatial structures, and multiplies these maps across layers (A_rollout = A_n * A_n-1 * ... * A_0). We then isolate the vector connecting to the global [CLS] token to see which image patches/words contributed most rigidly to the output.

Simplified Intuition: It visualizes the internal neural network's "eye-tracking". The heatmaps generated (like the red highlights below) actively prove that the AI isn't randomly guessing; it genuinely learns to focus on key facial expressions or pivotal vocabulary words to judge the film's genre.

Visualization Legend & Metrics Interpretation

Understanding the Inference Hardware Profiling:

  • Colors & Plots: The Purple histograms represent the baseline PyTorch execution latencies. The Red histograms showcase the highly optimized ONNX inference, highlighting a distinct shift left (significantly faster speeds!).
  • P50 (Median Latency): The 50th percentile metric. Exactly 50% of the inference requests finished faster than this time limit. This represents the "average" real-world speed experienced by half of the user base.
  • P90 (90th Percentile): 90% of requests successfully resolved under this duration. Architectures use this metric as the golden standard for guaranteed responsiveness.
  • P99 (Tail Latency): Resolves the absolute worst-case scenario. Guaranteeing only the slowest 1% of inferences exceed this threshold ensures real-time production systems do not suffer stuttering.
